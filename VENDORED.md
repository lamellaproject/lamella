## crates/lamella-tls-roots/src/roots.der and src/bundle.rs (2 files) -- generated 2026-07-12

- **Source:** the Mozilla CA certificate store as curl redistributes it, `https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem` (curl's
  extract of Mozilla NSS's `certdata.txt`): the bundle whose "Certificate data from Mozilla as of" line reads
  `Thu May 14 03:12:02 2026 GMT`. The SHA-256 of `roots.der` is
  `cf000d847f6d7383cf803e3cab28429d54625fc3f61075daf54e746dc3345e0b`.
- **Method:** generated by `lamella-tls-roots-gen` from that bundle. `roots.der` is every root certificate's DER,
  unchanged, concatenated in subject order; `bundle.rs` is the subject index over it, with the license notice. The
  generator's test checks that both files are exactly what it generates from the certificates in `roots.der`. They
  are regenerated, never edited by hand.
- **What it is:** the system root-certificate store that Lamella's device TLS backend trusts when firmware asks for
  system-root trust.
- **License:** `MPL-2.0`. See `crates/lamella-tls-roots/LICENSE-MPL` and the notice at the top of `bundle.rs`.
- **Modifications: NONE** to the certificates.
